Inclusion for All.
Opportunity for Every Ability.

Lakshya Foundation promotes disability inclusion through education access, assistive support, skill development, and awareness—ensuring equal dignity and participation for persons with disabilities.

Our Inclusion Programs Join as a Volunteer

Creating Accessible, Inclusive Communities

Disability should never be a barrier to education, employment, or dignity. We work to support persons with disabilities through accessible services, assistive aids, and community awareness.

Our initiatives focus on inclusion in schools, skill training for employment, access to assistive devices, and empowerment of families through counseling and support networks.

  • Assistive devices & rehabilitation support
  • Inclusive education and learning access
  • Skill training and livelihood opportunities

Screening, Early Identification & Referrals

Early identification helps people access the right support sooner. We conduct community screenings, connect families to services, and guide them through the next steps with care and dignity.

  • Community screenings with local networks and partners
  • Referral linkage to specialists, hospitals, and therapy centers
  • Family guidance for follow-ups, documentation, and support plans
  • Priority support for children and high-need cases

Assistive Devices & Support Services

Assistive devices can change lives. We help individuals access mobility aids, hearing and vision support, and rehabilitation guidance—building independence.

  • Mobility Aids such as wheelchairs and walkers
  • Hearing & Vision Support through access guidance
  • Rehabilitation Linkage for therapies and follow-ups
  • Family Counseling for continuous support

Our goal is to ensure every person has support to live with dignity and confidence.

Inclusive Education & Livelihoods

Inclusion means equal access to learning and opportunities. We support schools and communities to reduce barriers, and we provide skills training for sustainable livelihoods.

  • Inclusive Learning Support and accessible education awareness
  • Skill Training for employability and entrepreneurship
  • Job Linkages and livelihood support guidance
  • Community Awareness to reduce stigma and encourage inclusion
