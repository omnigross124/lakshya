CSR Partnerships

Creating Measurable Social Impact Together

Lakshya Foundation collaborates with corporates to design and implement high-impact, CSR-compliant programs aligned with Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013.

We offer end-to-end CSR execution — from need assessment and implementation to impact reporting and statutory audits.
