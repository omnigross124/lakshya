Accessible Healthcare for Healthier Communities

Lakshya Foundation is committed to closing the healthcare gap in underserved communities by delivering essential, affordable, and preventive healthcare services.

Through medical camps, health awareness programs, and early diagnostic support, we empower individuals and families to take charge of their health while strengthening local healthcare systems for long-term, sustainable impact.

  • Free medical check-ups & health camps
  • Preventive care & early diagnosis
  • Health education & community awareness

Mobile Medical Units

Access to healthcare should not depend on location. Our Mobile Medical Units travel to remote and underserved villages, delivering essential medical services directly to those who need them the most.

  • Qualified Doctor Consultations for primary healthcare needs
  • On-site Diagnostics including BP, sugar, and basic screenings
  • Free Medicines for common illnesses and chronic conditions
  • Health Awareness Sessions on hygiene, nutrition, and prevention

Through these units, we reduce travel burdens, ensure early diagnosis, and bring dignity and care to every doorstep.

Maternal Health Care

Every mother deserves a safe and healthy pregnancy. We provide continuous care, guidance, and support to ensure the wellbeing of both mother and child.

  • Antenatal Check-ups for early risk detection
  • Nutrition & Supplement Support for healthy development
  • Health Counseling for safe pregnancy and delivery
  • Postnatal Care for mother and newborn wellbeing
