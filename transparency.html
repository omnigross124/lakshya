Transparency

Lakshya Foundation is committed to openness, ethical governance, and responsible use of funds. We share key documents, policies, and reports to ensure donors, partners, and communities can trust our work.

Our Commitment

We believe transparency is not optional — it is the foundation of trust. We follow clear reporting, maintain compliance with applicable laws, and ensure programs are tracked through measurable outcomes.
