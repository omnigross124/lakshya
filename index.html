Empowering Communities, Transforming Lives

Lakshya Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to sustainable development across 12 key sectors, driving social change through education, healthcare, women empowerment, and community development initiatives.

Donate Now Volunteer With Us CSR Partnership

Our Key Focus Sectors

Education & Literacy

Providing quality education and literacy programs to underprivileged children and adults.

Healthcare & Public Health

Improving healthcare access and promoting public health awareness in rural areas.

Women Empowerment

Enabling women through skill development, entrepreneurship, and leadership programs.

Child Welfare

Protecting children's rights and ensuring their holistic development and safety.

Rural Development

Building sustainable rural communities through livelihood and infrastructure development.

Water & Sanitation

Ensuring access to clean water and promoting sanitation and hygiene practices.

Disaster Relief

Providing immediate assistance and long-term rehabilitation after natural disasters.

Disability Inclusion

Promoting inclusion and rehabilitation of persons with disabilities in all spheres.
