About Lakshya Foundation

Empowering communities through education, healthcare, and inclusive development.

Who We Are

Lakshya Foundation is a registered non-profit organization committed to building resilient communities through sustainable social development initiatives across India.

We work at the grassroots level to address critical challenges in education, healthcare, women empowerment, skill development, and community welfare. Our approach combines compassion, innovation, and measurable impact to create long-term change.

  • Empowering underprivileged communities
  • Promoting inclusive and sustainable growth
  • Partnering with institutions & volunteers
Learn More About Our Mission

Our Journey



Founded in 2005, Lakshya Foundation began as a small group of passionate volunteers driven by a shared belief in social equity, dignity, and opportunity for all.

What started as localized community outreach has grown into a multi-sector development initiative impacting lives across education, healthcare, women empowerment, and skill development. Our journey is rooted in grassroots action, sustained partnerships, and a commitment to measurable change.

2005

Humble Beginnings

Started with volunteer-led education support programs in underserved communities.

2010

Expanding Impact

Introduced healthcare awareness, sanitation drives, and women-focused initiatives.

2015

Structured Growth

Formalized partnerships with institutions, CSR programs, and local governments.

2020+

Nationwide Reach

Scaling sustainable models with technology, data-driven impact, and youth engagement.

Our Vision

A society where every individual lives with dignity, opportunity, and equitable access to essential resources.

At Lakshya Foundation, we envision communities that are self-reliant, inclusive, and empowered to shape their own futures. Our vision extends beyond immediate relief—focusing on long-term social transformation through education, health, livelihood, and equality.

  • Equal access to education and healthcare
  • Empowered women and youth-led communities
  • Sustainable development rooted in dignity

Our Mission

To implement community-led programs that create sustainable impact in education, healthcare, livelihoods, and social empowerment.

At Lakshya Foundation, our mission is rooted in collaboration with local communities. We design inclusive initiatives that strengthen capacities, promote self-reliance, and ensure long-term social transformation rather than short-term relief.

  • Strengthening access to quality education
  • Improving community health & nutrition
  • Creating livelihood and skill-building opportunities
  • Empowering women, youth, and marginalized groups

What We Do

Lakshya Foundation implements community-driven programs that address critical social challenges and create lasting impact across Maharashtra.

    Education: Improving access to quality education, digital learning, and skill development.
    Healthcare: Promoting preventive care, nutrition awareness, and primary health services.
    Women Empowerment: Supporting livelihoods, leadership, and financial independence.
    Child Welfare: Ensuring safety, education continuity, and holistic development.
    Rural Development: Strengthening livelihoods, sanitation, and sustainable village ecosystems.

Governance Structure

Lakshya Foundation is governed by a dedicated Board of Trustees and supported by an experienced Advisory Council that provides strategic guidance, ethical oversight, and policy direction.

Our governance framework ensures transparency, accountability, and regulatory compliance across all programs, financial operations, and stakeholder engagements.

  • 📜 Clearly defined roles & responsibilities
  • 🧾 Regular audits & statutory compliance
  • ⚖️ Strong ethical & risk management policies
  • 📊 Impact monitoring & performance reviews
