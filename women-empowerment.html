Our Women Programs Join as a Volunteer

Building Stronger Futures for Women & Girls

We believe women empowerment is the foundation of inclusive development. When women are educated, financially independent, and safe—entire families and communities thrive.

Through skill development, awareness programs, self-help groups, and leadership support, we enable women to access opportunities and break barriers—creating sustainable and long-term impact.

  • Skill training & livelihood support
  • Self-help groups & financial literacy
  • Rights, safety & health awareness

Livelihood & Entrepreneurship Support

Financial independence changes everything. Our programs support women with practical skills, confidence building, and entrepreneurship guidance to help them start earning and grow sustainably.

  • Skill Training in tailoring, crafts, digital & service skills
  • Micro-Enterprise Support for starting small businesses
  • Market Linkage to sell products and services locally
  • Mentorship to grow income and stability

When a woman earns, she invests in her family’s education, health, and future.

Women’s Health & Dignity

Empowerment also means health, dignity, and access to accurate information. We conduct sessions on menstrual hygiene, nutrition, mental wellbeing, and reproductive health—especially for young girls and women.

  • Menstrual Health awareness and hygiene support
  • Nutrition Guidance for strength and wellbeing
  • Mental Wellness sessions and counseling referrals
  • Maternal Support awareness and care guidance
